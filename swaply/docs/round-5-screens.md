# Round 5 screens, as exported

Machine-extracted from the round 5 design export (45 screens) on 09.09.2026, so
the implementation can be checked against the drawings rather than against
memory. Text only: every visible string in the order it appears, with `⟨bilde⟩`
where a photo sits.

**This file is the authority for screen content.** Where it disagrees with
`DESIGN.md`, the export wins and `DESIGN.md` is behind — that is the rule at the
top of `DESIGN.md` and it has already bitten several times here. Known deltas
the export forced:

- **Categories** are Sykling, Gaming, Verktøy, Klær, Båt, Friluft, Barn, Hjem,
  Sport, Musikk, Bøker, Diverse — not the list in the round 5 brief. There are
  subcategories too (Sykler, Elsykler, Elektroverktøy, Fiske…).
- **Login is email and password**, with Google, Facebook and Apple beside it.
  `DESIGN.md` said a magic link and nothing else.
- **The cash difference is paid over Vipps, between the two people.** The app
  shows the counterparty's number, a copy button, a link out, and a "mark as
  paid" — it never touches the money. Same shape for shipping: PostNord is a
  link out, and "mark as sent" / "mark as received" are self-reported.
- **Withdrawal is a negotiation of its own** (08a–08c): you ask, the other party
  answers within a deadline, and once something has been sent you cannot.
