## Platform

web

## Users

The user confirmed two primary audiences on 20.10.2026:

- **App developers and founders**, who are launching and marketing their own app.
- **Designers and agencies**, who show design and client work.

Marketers and web developers were offered as audiences and not chosen as primary.

## Product Purpose

PhoneMockup turns a screenshot or a screen recording into an animated 3D device mockup, either a short product film or a still. It runs in the browser and needs no 3D software. Success is a visitor going from their own screen to an exported video, GIF or still that they can post.

## Positioning

The device itself moves. PhoneMockup uses real, audited 3D models and sixteen editable camera motions, and renders and encodes them in the browser. It is free during beta, adds no watermark, and needs no account to start.

## Operating Context

- **The visitor's path.** Visitors arrive from search or from the homepage. They open the studio at `/platform/animation/<motion>`, drop in a screen, export, and post the result to a launch page, a store listing, a website or a social feed.
- **Devices.** The studio renders in a desktop browser. The marketing and guide pages must also work on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Devices.** Only four audited models are offered:
  - iPhone 16 Pro, with the Dynamic Island on or off;
  - Google Pixel 9 Pro, with the front camera cutout on or off;
  - Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra;
  - MacBook Pro 14″ (M4), with a lid angle from 0 to 130° and an option to open the lid during the animation.

  The manufacturer logo can be hidden on all four. The case keeps its own finish or takes a colour.
- **Screen.** The screen shows an image or a video. On Flip Cut, Tumble Cut and Whip Switch, a second image takes over the screen while the device faces away.
- **Motion.** There are 16 presets, grouped as Zoom, Spin, Flip, Cinematic, Reveal, Detail and Loop, each a 4–6 s loop. Every keyframe, its timing and its curve can be edited, or an animation can start from scratch.
- **Export.**
  - Motion exports as MP4, WebM, GIF or a PNG sequence. Stills export as PNG.
  - WebM, GIF and PNG keep a transparent background. MP4 does not.
  - Canvas sizes are 9:16 (2160×3840), 4:5 (2160×2700), 1:1 (2160×2160), 16:9 (3840×2160) and 21:9 (7680×3292).
- **Not offered:**
  - PSD or Figma files.
  - Batch export, which is marked "coming soon".
  - Cloud save. Projects are saved in the browser.
- **App Store previews.** Apple accepts only screen captures of the app itself as App Store app previews (App Review Guideline 2.3.4). PhoneMockup videos must not be sold as App Store previews.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name.** The product is called PhoneMockup. Its wordmark is "phonemockup" with a rust square. Production runs at https://phonemockup.app.
- **Price wording.** Confirmed 20.10.2026: say "Free during beta". Keep "during beta" while a price is still possible.
- **Competitors.** Confirmed 20.10.2026: comparisons with named competitors are allowed when they are factual and dated.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Preview footage.** Every motion has a real recording at `client/static/previews/motion-2026/<id>.mp4`, with a `.webp` poster.
- **Other assets.** A screenshot of the editor at `client/static/media/landing/editor.webp`, and the share image `client/static/og-cover.jpg`.
- **Missing proof.** There are no testimonials, customer logos, user counts, ratings or press. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the device moving before saying anything about it.
2. Every claim matches what the studio does today.
3. Every page is one click from the studio with the matching device and motion.
4. Be honest about PhoneMockup's limits and about competitors' strengths.
