# Podium — the live loop: display, ballot, admin

Scope: the three surfaces of the first build. The display and the ballot are
one moment, the admin is the desk it is built at. Display mode: Experience (the
slide leads, the tool recedes). Ballot and admin: Operate.

Decided without an ask round: the owner asked for no questions on 02.10.2026.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A vote lands where the presenter put it. Each answer option is a place
on the slide, and a vote is that place's count ticking up, heard and lit for a
moment. It refuses the category default, the results chart that replaces the
slide with bars.

OWN-WORLD: The counting room on election night. Dark ink ground for the room
(display and phone), cool off-white for the desk (admin), hairline rules
instead of cards, tabular figures for every count, one amber reserved for
change: a count that has just moved, and the live state. Nothing else is
coloured but what the presenter puts on a slide.

STORY: The presenter builds slides at a desk and places options like text. In
the room they press to the question; a QR code is up; phones scan and tap once;
the room hears each vote and watches the numbers climb in the places on the
slide.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Display, question slide: a 16:9 stage letterboxed on ink, the
question where it was placed, each option a placed box with its label and a
large tabular count over a hairline share bar, the QR and short address where
placed. No chrome. Ballot: the question at the top, the options as full-width
stacked targets at least 56 tall, «Stemt» stamped after.

FORM: Valgvaka, the election-night count; number 3 of seven (tally marks, DS
clinical white, valgvaka, stemmeseddel, split-flap, Swiss poster, scoreboard).
Seed b608fd81. Raises: a visible snap grid in the editor (from the Crouwel
specimen, competitive); one change colour that holds until the count settles
(from the gate board, competitive); tabular figures at density for every count
(from Ikeda, declined).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
